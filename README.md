### 🔭 Research Interests & Long-term Focus

My long-term research interest lies in **Multimodal Spatiotemporal Intelligence & Systems Engineering**, with a particular focus on connecting heterogeneous sensing, machine learning, and reliable real-world systems.

I'm especially curious about:

- 🧭 **Multimodal Sensor Fusion & Spatial Intelligence** — IMU, UWB, wireless localisation, computer vision, audio, and cross-modal fusion.
- 🏃 **Human Motion & Behaviour Understanding** — wearable sensing, motion recognition, spatiotemporal modelling, and human-centred intelligence.
- 🧠 **Machine Learning & Statistical Intelligence** — probabilistic modelling, causal inference, representation learning, and robust evaluation.
- ⚙️ **Data & Intelligent Systems Engineering** — data pipelines, multimodal infrastructure, system reliability, and deployment.
- 📈 **Decision Science & Optimisation** — forecasting, experimentation, resource allocation, and data-driven decision-making.

I'm interested in how these ideas can translate into systems that work beyond controlled environments.
